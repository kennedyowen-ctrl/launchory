Launchory's fee: 7%

Founders pay for accepted proposals through Stripe Checkout. Launchory retains a 7% platform commission; the creative receives 93% before applicable Stripe fees. Launchory records payment and transfers the creative share after founder approval of delivered work, subject to payment availability and Stripe rules. This is not escrow.
